## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a hyper-modern, analytical fintech interface engineered for traders, institutional analysts, and retail investors who require real-time market comprehension. The visual ethos combines crisp Scandinavian minimalism with the dense utility of high-frequency trading terminals.

The aesthetic philosophy is defined by:
- **Utilitarian Clarity**: Zero-friction data visualization where negative space is calibrated to emphasize metrics, delta changes, and hierarchical market depth.
- **Instrument-Grade Precision**: Razor-thin structural borders (`#E0E3EB`), monospaced alignment for numerical values, and micro-elevation cues replace heavy gradients and ornamental styling.
